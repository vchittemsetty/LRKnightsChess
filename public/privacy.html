Privacy Policy – LR Knights Chess Tournament App Effective Date: August 29, 2025 Introduction LR Knights Chess Tournament App (“the App”) is designed to manage chess tournaments, including player registration, pairings, standings, and results. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information collected through the App. Information We Collect Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we collect your email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) as provided by Google. Tournament Data: Player names, sections, ratings, pairings, and results are stored in Google Sheets associated with the tournament. How We Use Information To authenticate Tournament Directors (TDs) who manage pairings and results. To display tournament pairings, results, and standings for participants and parents. To ensure fair play and accurate record keeping. We do not sell, share, or use your personal data for advertising. Data Storage Tournament data (players, pairings, results) is stored in Google Sheets within the TD’s Google account. Access to modify tournament data is restricted to authenticated TDs. Public viewers may see tournament pairings and standings, but not private account information. Sharing of Information We do not share your personal information with third parties except: As required by law, or With explicit consent from the Tournament Director. Security We use Google’s authentication and data security features. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection. Children’s Privacy This App may be used in scholastic tournaments involving children. Player names and results may be published as part of standings, but no personal data beyond the tournament context is collected. Parents may request removal of their child’s name from published standings by contacting the Tournament Director. Your Rights You may: Request deletion of your Google sign-in data from the App. Decline to provide information (though this may limit participation in tournament management).
